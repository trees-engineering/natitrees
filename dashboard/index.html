Treelance AI Voice Agent
An outbound AI recruiter that calls candidates, holds a genuine conversation, and builds a rich profile — all without a human in the loop.
What it does
The Mission
Treelance makes outbound calls to candidates in the Trees OS talent network. It acts as a warm, curious AI connector — not a screener. It listens far more than it talks, follows emotional energy, and builds a picture of who the candidate really is, so matching becomes faster and more relevant.
Identity: Who is Treelance?
The agent introduces itself honestly as an AI and represents Trees OS. It holds a warm, unhurried conversation — never scripted, never extractive.
Warm & Curious Honest about being AI Speaks 20% — Listens 80% Multilingual Barge-in support
Call Flow
01
Trigger
POST /call with a talentId. Candidate profile is loaded from Supabase. Twilio places the outbound call.
02
Answer & Greet
When the candidate picks up, the agent greets them by name, discloses it's an AI, and checks if now is a good time.
03
Live Conversation Loop
Candidate speech → Deepgram STT → Gemini LLM → ElevenLabs TTS → back to Twilio. Runs in real-time with barge-in support.
04
Tool Calls (Phase 2)
During the call, Gemini can invoke tools — check availability, book calendar slots with human recruiters.
05
Natural Close
When the conversation reaches a natural end, the agent closes warmly and appends [END_CALL] to trigger hang-up.
06
Save & Process
Full transcript and AI summary are saved to Supabase _assessments table for recruiter review.
